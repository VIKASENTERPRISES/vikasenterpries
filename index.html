Solar Systems

☀️ ON-GRID

Panels → grid-tie inverter → home/shop loads → surplus to grid through applicable net metering.

Best for: Areas with reliable grid supply and bill reduction goals.

🔋 HYBRID

Solar + hybrid inverter + battery + grid for backup and reduced grid dependence.

Best for: Homes and businesses needing reliable backup.

🔌 OFF-GRID

Panels → charge/inverter → battery → loads. Designed for locations without suitable grid supply.

Best for: Remote farms and independent power requirements.
